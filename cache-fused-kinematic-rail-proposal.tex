\section{Introduction and Motivation}
The prevailing paradigm in AI safety treats alignment as a post-hoc correction mechanism. Techniques such as Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), external safety classifiers, and API-based meta-governors operate on the generated text, introducing a fatal detection lag. By fusing reflection tokens directly into the KV-cache at the kernel level, we transition from temporary software stents to permanent geometric grafts, rendering external API meta-governors and $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$ memory spikes obsolete.

HOW TO READ THE TABLE
  best_auc    AUROC of the best feature in its best direction (two-sided; always >= 0.5)
  p_max       best-feature permutation p (already corrected for the feature search)
  p_max_len   same, shuffling within length bins (signal beyond length)
  p_max_holm  Holm across all cells in the sample mode
  n_fdr       features with per-feature permutation FDR < 0.10; compare with n_eff.
              Hundreds of 'hits' with n_eff of a few means one effect repeated, or an
              artifact shared by all features (length, termination, template).
Patterns
  hardened/massif significant, rule/massif too   -> feature-family effect survives a label change
  only rule labels significant (either family)   -> label-set effect; treat the v2 result as an artifact
  delta significant under rule labels as well    -> the 'variance diagonal was wrong' claim is untested
  nothing significant under hardened labels      -> no replication on the better labels
Also check length_auc (signed raw AUROC of length) and p_max_len. With ~25-50 items only AUROCs above ~0.75 are detectable.
